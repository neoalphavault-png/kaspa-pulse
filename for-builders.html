Last updated on 7 October 2026.

Who we are

Kaspa Pulse counts what happens on the Kaspa chain, independently, and publishes the method with every number so anyone can repeat it. We take paid work from teams building on Kaspa under the same rule. You get the count and the method, never an endorsement.

Three things we do

Pre-audit review

A read of your project before it goes to a professional audit, with a written list of what we found. One round covers one version of the project.

150 € per round

On-chain count

A count on the Kaspa chain that you define with us, done the way we do our own, with the method written down so you or anyone else can check it.

from 75 €

Testnet load measurement

We put load on your application on Kaspa testnet and measure how it holds up.

250 € for one load curve
450 € for two or three scenarios
150 € per month for ongoing measurement

All prices are net, plus VAT where it applies.

What this is not

  1. Not a security audit. A pre-audit review does not replace one and does not say your project is safe.
  2. No "reviewed by" badge. You cannot use our name to say your project was checked or approved.
  3. No token promotion. No posts, rankings or mentions for payment. Paid work never changes what we count or publish on kaspapulse.com.
  4. Testnet only for load tests. We never put test load on Kaspa mainnet.

We do not name the teams we work for and we do not publish their addresses.
