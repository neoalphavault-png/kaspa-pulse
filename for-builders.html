Why now

Kaspa covenants have been live on mainnet since 30 June 2026, and new apps ship every week. The cheapest moment to find a problem is before anyone's money touches it. An audit checks your code. It won't tell you that your fee line hides a cost or that your simple mode isn't simple.

What we typically find

  • A Kaspa wallet or app review screen that shows a total without the protocol fee in it.
  • Test or debug options that look like choices for the user.
  • Beginner modes full of words beginners don't know.
  • Limits that make the user do math instead of offering a max.
