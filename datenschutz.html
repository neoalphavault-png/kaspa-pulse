Privacy Policy

Datenschutzerklärung · Last updated: October 2026

1. Controller

Vizora Digital UG (haftungsbeschränkt)
Marc-Chagall-Straße 54
40477 Düsseldorf, Germany
Email: hello@kaspapulse.com

kaspapulse.com is an informational site about the Kaspa network (on-chain data, market data, and a weekly newsletter). This policy explains what personal data is processed when you use it, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, GitHub collects technically necessary data (in particular your IP address) in server logs in order to deliver the website and keep the service secure. We have no access to these logs and do not analyze them.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing the website securely and efficiently). GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Live data from third-party APIs (DefiLlama, CoinGecko)

To display current market and on-chain data, your browser fetches data directly from the APIs of DefiLlama (api.llama.fi) and CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com) when the page loads. For technical reasons, your IP address is transmitted to these providers' servers in the process. No other data is shared, and no cookies are set.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in displaying live data, which is the core purpose of this site).

4. Newsletter (Brevo)

We use Brevo, a service of Brevo GmbH, Köpenicker Straße 126, 10179 Berlin, Germany, to send our weekly newsletter. When you subscribe via the form, your email address is transmitted to and stored by Brevo.

Subscription uses a double opt-in process: after signing up you receive a confirmation email, and you are only added to the list once you click the confirmation link. Your email address is used exclusively to send the newsletter and is not shared with any other third parties.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6 (1)(a) GDPR). You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every email; your data will then be deleted from the mailing list. More information: Brevo Privacy Policy.

5. SSL encryption

This website uses SSL/TLS encryption ("https://" in the address bar). Data you submit to us — such as your email address when subscribing to the newsletter — cannot be read by third parties in transit.

6. Cookies and web analytics (Plausible)

This website sets no cookies. Since 26 October 2026 we count visits with Plausible Analytics, a service of Plausible Insights OÜ, Västriku tn 2, 50403 Tartu, Estonia. Plausible works without cookies and stores no personal data. Your IP address and browser identifier are only used to tell visitors apart within one day, as a hash with a salt that is replaced every 24 hours, and are never stored. The data is processed on servers in the EU.

What we see are totals per day, such as the pages viewed, the referring website or campaign, the country, the device type and the browser. When a newsletter signup succeeds, the page also sends a signup event with the page path and the campaign name of the link you came from. Your email address is never part of it.

The purpose is to learn which pages and campaigns are useful to readers and lead to newsletter signups. No profiles are built and nothing is shared with other third parties. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in measuring the reach of our site without tracking individuals. More information is in the Plausible data policy.

7. Your rights

Regarding your personal data, you have the right to:

  • access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • withdraw consent with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3) GDPR)

To exercise any of these rights, simply email hello@kaspapulse.com.

8. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia (LDI NRW), www.ldi.nrw.de. You may also contact the supervisory authority of your own EU member state.

9. Changes

We may update this privacy policy if the legal situation or this website changes. The current version published here always applies.
