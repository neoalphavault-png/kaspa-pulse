How to read the result

  1. Open this page with parameters, for example ?utm_source=yt-pinned&utm_medium=shorts&utm_campaign=week-in-30.
  2. The three values above must show what you put in the address.
  3. Send with a test address, then open that contact in Brevo.
  4. If the three attributes are filled at the contact, Brevo accepts the fields and the live form is already sending them too.
  5. If they are empty, the three fields are missing inside the Brevo form. Five clicks fix that, see utm-regel.md in pulse-studio.
