how we count
price, the close and the two moving averages come from kraken's daily candles, weekly marks every sunday 00:00 utc, 103 full weeks from 24 november 2024 checked for gaps. the 21 week average is the exponential average of the last 21 weekly closes, seeded with the plain average of the first 21; the 50 week average is the plain average of the last 50 weekly closes. the chart above draws from that same series. block reward and emission come from our own chain reader against kaspa's fixed anchor and monthly step; for the next cut we print the date only, because the step lands on a block score and not on a clock, so the minute moves. hashrate is our own daily reading, averaged over seven days per window. the wallet figures are two readings at the times given.
