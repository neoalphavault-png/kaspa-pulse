how we count
price, the close and the two moving averages come from kraken's daily candles, weekly marks every sunday 00:00 utc, 96 full weeks checked for gaps. the 21 and 50 week averages are our own rolling calculation, seeded from the first weeks of the window; the chart above draws straight from that same series. block reward, emission and mined percent come from our own chain reader against kaspa's fixed anchor and monthly step. transactions per second are the kaspalytics weekly reading of monday 21 sep, standard transfers only, averaged over the seven days to that mark; it does not cover this week. the fee share is our own count across 1 to 25 september, fees and reward over the same 25 days, because the fee feed itself has produced no fresh daily reading since 17 august.
