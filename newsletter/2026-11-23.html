how we count
token holders come from our own kaspa node. once a day at 12:00 utc we read every KCC-20 output that is still unspent, prove each token's state by its hash back to its genesis, and count unique addresses per token and across all tokens. a holder is an address with a nonzero balance, not a person. amounts are in the token's own units with its own decimals, no dollar value is attached. price, the close and the two moving averages come from kraken's daily candles, weekly marks every sunday 00:00 utc, 104 full weeks from 24 november 2024. the 21 week average is the exponential average of the last 21 weekly closes, seeded with the plain average of the first 21; the 50 week average is the plain average of the last 50 weekly closes. block reward and emission come from our own chain reader against kaspa's fixed anchor and monthly step; for the next cut we print the date only, because the step lands on a block score and not on a clock. hashrate is our own daily reading, averaged over seven days per window. the wallet figures are two readings at the times given.
