## Counting rules, unchanged from COUNT.md

- **Rate:** only the hops inside a step, divided by its length. Hops after the close are the tail and are listed apart. A ramp step is its 50 s on.
- **Line:** 1.14 per second for the whole step, 1,026 accepted hops per family for 900 s and 3,078 for 2,700 s. No minute is marked as passing or failing.
- **Near the line:** a family within 10 hops of the line is marked, with its hops in the first and the last second of the step shown apart. We show it and do not decide.
- **Clock:** the header time of the accepting block.
- **Kept apart:** late landings after 22:00 UTC, genesis and conflicting spends.
