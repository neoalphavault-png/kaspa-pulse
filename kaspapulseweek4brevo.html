THE TAKE
hashrate jumped 31% in one week and took back everything it lost. the reward pool did not grow with it. more machines splitting the same emission means revenue per petahash is down to roughly $161 a day, and wednesday's reward cut takes it to about $152. so each machine earns less this week, and wednesday makes it less again. holders did the opposite. over half of all kas has now sat still for a year, holder addresses grew again, and the exchange addresses we track sent out 85.2 million more kas than they took in.
WORTH WATCHING
two chains, opposite jobs. igra now carries 49,237 of the 50,307 daily l2 transactions, which is 98% of everything happening. but trading goes the other way. kasplex does $99K of the $109K daily dex volume while igra does $10K. one chain is where things happen, the other is where things get priced. we do not know yet whether that gap closes or hardens.
HOW WE COUNT
we changed two definitions this week and we would rather tell you than quietly restate. our covenant number was counting events, it now counts transactions. our active address number was being read mid day, it is now always the last complete day. both old series looked like growth and were not. we cleared both series rather than carry a fake trend, so the dashboard shows a gap where the old numbers used to be.
